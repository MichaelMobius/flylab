# FlyLab v0.6 — Neural Inspector

## Visual / anatomical
- More anatomical procedural fly with halteres, articulated multi-segment legs with tarsi, improved wing silhouette/veins and compound-eye facet highlights.
- Procedural fruit skin textures, orange dimpling, banana freckles, lobed apple geometry and larger readable fruit scale.
- Silver/light UI retained and refined around the immersive terrarium.

## Neural inspection
- Click the fly or use the Neural control to enable camera follow.
- Right-button orbit and wheel zoom remain active while following.
- Floating inspector shows action, dominant region, six region bars, functional brain map and recent time trace.
- AL/MB remain derived from the current controller; CX/SEZ/DN/VNC remain explicitly marked proxies.

## Feeding
- Fly visually settles onto fruit and extends its proboscis.
- Fruit shrinks and signals low remaining food before removal at zero.
